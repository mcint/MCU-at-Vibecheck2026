Sources and assumptions
Federal rule: IRS PTC eligibility Q&A says the 2026 income limit is 400% FPL; 2021–2025 temporarily had no upper cutoff. The same page says the applicable poverty guideline is the most recently published one at Marketplace open enrollment. For 2026 enrollment, this is the 2025 contiguous-state guideline: $15,650 for one person.
Contribution: IRS Revenue Procedure 2025-25 sets 9.96% for the 300–400% band. The draft 2026 Form 8962 rounds annual and monthly contributions to whole dollars. At $62,600: annual contribution $6,235; monthly $520. Against the representative $661 monthly benchmark, the modeled monthly credit is $141. At $62,601 the federal credit ends.
Premium: KFF's 2026 Texas average benchmark premium is $661/month. KFF describes this measure as the second-lowest-cost Silver premium for a 40-year-old, weighted across counties by plan selections. It is a state average, not Maya's purchasable local quote.
Assume a one-person tax family, single filer not claimed as a dependent, enrolled all year in the benchmark Marketplace plan with no affordable employer or government coverage. This is an illustrative scenario, not Maya's personal enrollment quote. The calculation models monthly federal credit and premium only. It excludes ordinary income and payroll taxes, individual county/plan prices, state programs, cost-sharing reductions, life changes and final tax-return reconciliation. A $1 annual MAGI change could follow from a $1 raise if other income adjustments stay fixed. The phase-out is hypothetical, not current law. Lean proves this encoded calculation, not personal eligibility or final tax liability.
